TrafficVision-AI Research Notes
===============================

IMPLEMENTED
-----------
- A standalone Python workspace was created for motorcycle helmet-detection research and model experimentation.
- It has no dependency on the Flask/React application or its application-facing ml/ modules.
- Read-only future tools are available for paired-YOLO dataset inspection, duplicate reporting, group-exclusive splitting, YOLO training, evaluation, and training-history visualization.
- Inspection and duplicate tools report issues but never delete, move, or edit source data.

DATASET PENDING

- Add the future annotated dataset under data/annotated/. Confirm its final layout and annotation format before use.
- Provisional expected classes, subject to confirmation: no_helmet (0), helmet (1).

RESEARCH PLAN
-------------
- Current objective: robust motorcycle helmet detection as the core detector for the planned integrated dual-camera violation-detection research direction.
- Before splitting, identify video/sequence/source groups and duplicates. Each group must wholly belong to train, validation, or test to prevent leakage from neighbouring frames.
- Audit label validity and class balance before creating a versioned split.
- Record real experiments as EXP-001, EXP-002, etc., including config, dataset/split version, model, hyperparameters, seed, history, metrics, checkpoints, and notes.

FUTURE EXPERIMENT
-----------------
- Establish the baseline only after data audit and reviewed group-based splitting.
- Rider-vehicle association, confidence-aware temporal verification, and dual-camera synchronization are future work and are not implemented here.
